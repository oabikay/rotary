About Us

An overview of our organization

Brief history about our amiable organization


Rotary Club Isolo Metropolitan was founded in the 2018–2019 Rotary year by a group of vibrant and passionate humanitarians, committed to making a lasting impact in their community and beyond.

The club began its journey under the inspiring leadership of Charter President Rtn. Gbenga Shodunke, whose vision set the tone for excellence in service.

Since then, the mantle of leadership has been carried with distinction by Rtn. Edward Quadri, Rtn. Wale Olanniyo, Rtn. Gbenga Osasanmi, and Rtn. Kunle Adeshina—each building on the legacy of service and community development.

Today, the club is proudly led by its first and only female President, the dynamic and Irresistible Rtn. Funks Shodunke, who continues to drive the club forward with passion, purpose, and an unwavering commitment to Rotary ideals.

Rotary Club Isolo Metropolitan remains a beacon of hope, service, and fellowship in the heart of Lagos.

Our Mission

To unite passionate professionals and community leaders in service, fostering sustainable development and positive change through impactful humanitarian projects, fellowship, and leadership.






Our Vision

To be a leading Rotary Club known for excellence in service, innovation in impact, and commitment to building stronger, healthier, and more empowered communities in Isolo and beyond.






Our Value

  • SERVICE
    We are dedicated to serving others, putting community needs above self.
  • LEADERSHIP
    We lead by example and empower members to create lasting change.
  • INTEGRITY
    We uphold high ethical standards in all we do.
  • FELLOWSHIP
    We build lifelong friendships and strong professional networks through Rotary.
  • DIVERSITY
    We respect and value differences, fostering an inclusive environment.
  • EXCELLENCE
    We strive for excellence in service, leadership, and impact.
